Pointer settings, step by step

Hold the ↓ and ↑ thumbs the whole time (ADJUST). Hover a row to see the keys: blue = hold, orange = tap. Each tap changes the value by a small step, so tap 5–10 times to feel it. The LEDs confirm every step on the half whose device changed: white = up, red = down (left = trackpoint, right = touchpad). No flash means the keys didn't register as an adjustment.
